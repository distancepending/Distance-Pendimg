JOANIE CARDOZA

For most of the time I knew Joanie Cardoza, she wasn't “someone who was sick.” She was just Mama Joanie.

She fought lymphoma for years. I'm taking on the Daytona 100 in her memory and raising $50,000 for Blood Cancer United through Team In Training.

“
My mom. The fightiest fighter there ever was.

It took cancer three tries to take her down, and even then, she sure as hell didn't make it easy. She persevered with incredible strength, fierce determination, and as much dignity as anyone could possibly muster through the absolute hell that is cancer.

She taught us to fight hard, love even harder, and never underestimate the strength of a woman who refuses to give up.

Cancer may have taken her from us, but it will never take away the love she gave, the lessons she taught, or the fire she left in all of us.

— Suzanna, Joanie's daughter TURN HER STORY INTO ACTION →
