THE MISSION

What are Team In Training & Blood Cancer United?

The 100 miles are the challenge. This is what the challenge is for.

Team In Training

Team In Training is Blood Cancer United's endurance sports fundraising program. Since beginning in 1988, more than 700,000 people have joined the Team and raised over $2 billion to help advance blood cancer research and support patients and families.

I'm participating through TNT's DIY program: choose the challenge, set the goal, and use the miles to make an impact.

LEARN ABOUT TEAM IN TRAINING →

Blood Cancer United

Blood Cancer United works to cure blood cancer and improve the lives of patients and families through research, patient education and support, financial assistance, and policy advocacy.

It is the largest nonprofit funder of blood cancer research and has invested more than $2 billion in research.

THE CHALLENGE

What is the Daytona 100?

100 MILES. ONE COASTLINE. ON FOOT.

The Daytona 100 is a point-to-point pavement ultramarathon along Florida's S.R. A1A—the Ocean Road. The 100-mile race begins at Jacksonville Beach and follows Florida's East Coast barrier island to the finish at the Ponce Inlet Lighthouse, just south of Daytona Beach.

The route parallels the Atlantic Ocean and Intracoastal Waterway for the entire distance. The 2026 race takes place December 12–13.

WHY DISTANCE PENDING?

You rarely know how far you actually have to go.

In running, you can know the mileage on paper without knowing what those miles will ask of you. Life works the same way. Looking at the entire distance can feel impossible.

But you never have to cover the whole distance at once. You only have to deal with the step in front of you. That's the idea behind Distance Pending.

“The distance is pending.
Just the next step.”
